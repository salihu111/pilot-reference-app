Addis FIR Escape Routes from exit points   
  
KONET  
DP Route: WITH IN 115 DME GWZ  
DESCEND 14,000FT TURN TO THE WEST AND INTERCEPT R-350 GWZ AND  
PROCEED TO HAAB. BEYOND 115 DESCEND 17,000FT TURN TO THE EAST AND INTERCEPT R-170 QHA AND FOLLOW ASOLE DP ROUTE  
  
TORBA/LAKBE   
DP Route:West of position ASOLE, initially descent and maintain FL140 intercept ADS Radial  
080 then Descent to 12500ft on R-080 to 25 miles then direct to AB111 for  
straight in approach RWY 25L/R or fly direct to KALTY from AB111 for RNAV RWY  
07R/L.East of position ASOLE and West of DTI, descent and maintain FL125 and proceed  
direct to (ADS R-080/100 miles) then follow the above procedure  
  
ARSHI  
 DP Route: ASOLE  
MAV  
 DP Route: ALEMU  
  
ALEMU  
DP Route: North of ALEMU, descend initially FL140 turn and intercept airway UG650 (UM308)  
(R-193 ADS). Proceed direct ARONA for RNAV (GPS) RWY 07R/L or descend FL125 proceed  
to along the airway to ADS VOR. From ADS VOR commence approach to RWY 25L/R to Addis  
Ababa (HAAB).South of ALEMU descend FL100 and proceed to Mombasa (HKMO) along the airway to MOV  
VOR for ILS/RNAV RWY 21 or RNAV/VOR RWY 03  
  
RUDOL  
DP Route:North of RUDOL, descend initially to FL130 proceed to Addis Ababa along the  
airway to OKNET. From OKNET descend FL125, Intercept R-193 inbound ADS VOR.  
From ADS VOR commence approach to RWY 25L/R or fly direct ARONA for RNAV 07R/L to  
Addis Ababa (HAAB).South of RUDOL, descend initially FL120 proceed to Nairobi along the airway to  
LOTAS. From LOTAS proceed to NAK VOR then to GG NDB/GV VOR or NV VOR descend  
FL100 for RWY 06 to Nairobi (HKJK)  
  
EKBUL  
DP Route:North East of EKBUL descent and maintain FL140 proceed along the airway to ETGOM  
From ETGOM descend FL135 Intercept Radial 193 inbound ADS VOR then descend to FL 125  
From ADS VOR commence an approach Runway 25L/R or fly direct to ARONA/AWASH for RNAV  
07R/L to ADDIS (HAAB)South West of EKBUL Descend to FL100 proceed along the airway to PATAR.  
Remain on the airway centerline or Northwest of airway then, from PATAR to NABRO, PB  
or NN VOR.From PB NDB or NN VOR commence approach ILS RWY17 to Entebbe (HUEN).  
  
DAGAP  
DP Route:GWZ - UB535 - DAGAP (Addis Ababa)  
North East of DAGAP descend to FL135 proceed along the airway UB535 to KOFTA then  
fly direct to ARONA/AWASH for RNAV approach Runway 07R/L Or to ADS VOR, commence  
approach RWY 25L/R Addis Ababa (HAAB).  
North West of DAGAP turn and fly direct to KTM and descend FL100 and proceed to  
Khartoum (HSSS) RWY 18/36 via KTM VOR.  
  
BRO  
DP Route: East of EGBIM Descend to FL135 proceed along the airway UT139 to GUDER then fly  direct to AWASH/ARONA for RNAV approach RWY 07R/L Or  
 to ADS VOR, commence approach RWY 25L/R Addis Ababa (HAAB).   
West of EGBIM turn and direct to KTM then descend to FL100  proceed to Khartoum  (HSSS) RWY 18/36 via KTM VOR.   
  
ALRAP  
GWZ - UR611 (UN301) - TIKAT and GWZ – UT124 – ALRAP (Addis Ababa)         
Between GWZ and TIKAT/ALRAP, turn and intercept R-312 GWZ inbound to position LIBAS  (N09°31'00 E038°11'00 on Radial/DME 305/42 GWZ VOR) descend and maintain FL135. From LIBAS proceed direct to AWASH for RNAV approach RWY 07R/L Addis Ababa (HAAB).Or Direct ADS VOR and commence approach to RWY 25L/R Addis Ababa (HAAB).
